What to include

A few details up front help us give you a useful answer the first time.

  • What you needA short description of the problem or project, in your own words.
  • What you're usingDevices, software or services involved, such as Microsoft 365, a router or a camera system.
  • Business or homeWhether this is for a company or a household, and roughly how many people or devices.
  • How to reach youYour name and the best way and time to get back to you.

What happens next

No jargon and no pressure. Here is how a first conversation goes.

  1. You send a message

    Tell us what you need. Rough notes are fine, and you don't need to know the technical terms.

  2. We talk it through

    We ask a few questions to understand your setup, what's not working and what you want to achieve.

  3. You get a clear plan

    We explain what we recommend and what the next steps are, so you can decide how to move forward.
