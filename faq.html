Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what people ask us most. Don't see yours? Just email us.

Where do you work?

We're based in Merrimack, NH and serve southern New Hampshire in person. Many problems can be fixed with remote support, so you often don't need a visit. See our service area for details.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. You don't need a business to get IT help. We work with businesses of all sizes and with everyday people at home, on things like security cameras, Wi-Fi, device setup and general tech support.

How much do you charge?

Labor is $50 per hour for work at home and $100 per hour for businesses. Businesses that want ongoing support can add a $100 monthly plan, which takes 10% off all labor. Full details are on the pricing page.

What does the monthly business plan include?

The monthly business plan is $100 per month and takes 10% off all labor. You can add Microsoft 365 management, firewall management and remote access to build the plan around what you need.

What are the add-ons, and what do they cost?

Microsoft 365 management is $25 per month plus $10 per user. Firewall management is $50 per month. Remote access is $10 per endpoint. EDR is $8 per endpoint.

What is EDR?

EDR stands for endpoint detection and response. It's security software that watches each computer for threats and helps stop them. It's available as an add-on at $8 per endpoint per month.

Can you manage our Microsoft 365?

Yes. We handle users and licenses, email, security settings and ongoing support, so your team can focus on their work.

Do you install security cameras?

Yes. We plan, install and set up cameras around your home, and show you how to view them from your phone. Email us a short description of what you'd like covered and we'll take it from there.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes. With remote access set up on a computer, we can often help without coming to you. Remote access is $10 per endpoint.

How do I get started?

Send us an email at info@latency-lab.com, or use the buttons on the contact page to start a message. Tell us what's going on and we'll talk it through with you.
